Overview

About the project.

The intranet is a internal order-management platform, designed to improve daily workflows and task management.

The company I worked for needed to replace an old LAMP platfrm with an updated system that would allow them to manage their orders and customers more efficiently. I was tasked with building the new intranet from scratch, using raw html, css and javascript.

The amount of issues this platform solved for the company was incredible. It allowed them to manage their orders more efficiently, and it also allowed them to track their sales and revenue more accurately. The intranet was a huge success, and it was a pleasure to work on this project.

Shipping section of the intranet, where shippers can export all the orders that need to be shipped, as well as a breakdown of partial shipped items.

Interesting parts.

Customer Dashboard

This project required me to create two dashboards for the system, one used internally and one externally. The external dashboard is for customers to edit their products and export spreadsheets of their orders.

02

API Functionality

Customers are able to integrate their system with ours, with the built-in API functionality. This allows them to automatically update their orders and products, as well as export their orders to our system.

03

Billing & Inventory

The internal dashboard allows the company to manage their orders and customers more efficiently. It also allows them to track their sales and revenue more accurately, as well as manage their shipping and inventory.
