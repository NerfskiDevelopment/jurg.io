Trackle

[2026]

Trackle is a .NET Web/Mobile application that serves as a delivery-list and shipment-tracker for customers of a local delivery service. It allows customers to receive notifications when their deliveries are on the way or have been delivered. The application also provides an mobile interface for delivery drivers to take photos and signatures, for proof of delivery.
